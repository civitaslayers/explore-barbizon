## Other details

- Division plan, August 1991 (J.A. Chetaud, géomètre, Dammarie-lès-Lys), annexes 1 pp. 10-18: SFPP property on AK 51, 52, 54, 308, 309, 310, 5,764 m²; street building R+1+C; well on the south boundary; neighbours M. Jacques Schaller (adjoining), Mme Henri Bobin, M. et Mme Guy Faubert, Leroux, "Mademoiselle Claudine" (surname cut).
- Arrêté de voirie, 13 Oct 2008, annexes 1 p. 32: cites the plan d'alignement of rue Antoine Barye annexed to an arrêté préfectoral of 16 Oct 1908 (repères 16, 18, 20).
- Certificat d'urbanisme 7 Aug 2008 lists AK 326 (1,140 m²), absent from the deed; probably an error for AK 325. Unchecked.
- Mme Marinette Cailleret occupied part of the building at the 2008 sale. Same surname as Adrienne Cailleret: probable relative, unverified.
- Servitude (1991 deed): pedestrian passage over AK 314 for hotel clients only, valid only while the building is used as hôtel-restaurant-bar; no fence over about 21 m from the street.

## Resolved by these deeds

- Émile Schaller's wife, "Marguerite" in the 1901-1911 censuses and "Adrienne" in 1921, is one person: Adrienne Marguerite Charlotte Cailleret.
- Spelling: Hélène Rosa ROBINOT.
