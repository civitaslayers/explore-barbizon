# Barbizon censuses 1891-1926: Bodmer and Schaller households (browser extension report, 2026-10-12)

Report from the Claude browser extension in Luigi's Chrome, AD77 online census registers, read on page images. Committed verbatim.

I went through the Barbizon pages of all six censuses on the page images. No Bodmer appears at or next to the Schaller hotel in any year I could read. The only time a Bodmer and the Schaller hotel are both in the Barbizon lists, in 1911, they are on different streets, 65 houses apart.

Three things about the registers themselves come first:

- **1891, 1896 and 1901** are filed under **Chailly-en-Bière**, because Barbizon only became its own commune in 1903. Barbizon is its own section at the end of each list.
- **1926**: the Barbizon file (10M507, viewer 941884) holds only the cover and summary page, image 0032. That page totals 231 maisons and 730 individuals. The next image is Dammarie-lès-Lys, so the name lists for 1926 are not in this file.
- **1891 has a gap.** Image 0185 ends at line 780 (maison 210, ménage 247), and image 0186 starts at line 841 (maison 223, ménage 265). One double page is not in the file.

The viewer page number is the image number minus the first image of the file, plus one. Columns are shown as maison / ménage / line.

**On side of the street:** no register says which side a house stands on. The 1911 street labels are the only direct evidence, and they put the two households on different streets.

**On annotations:** there is no "Charmettes", "pavillon", "annexe" or "chez Schaller" anywhere. No Bodmer is listed as a Schaller employee, lodger or member of the hotel household. In 1906 and 1911 the hotel staff name Schaller as employer.

**One limit to flag:** I found the names by scanning the name columns at reading size, then read only the households above in full at 2x. Faint spellings elsewhere could have been missed.

**Possible conflict with earlier work:** I previously noted that Landerer appears 1876-1891. The 1891 entry is confirmed above, aged 74.
