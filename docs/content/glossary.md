# Glossary (termbase)

Last updated: 2026-10-18. Companion to `docs/content/writing-guide.md`.

Use these forms exactly. Add a term before using a new one twice. French is the
reference column. Columns marked "to confirm" have not been checked by a native
reader and must not be published until they are.

Rules:
- Place names (streets, houses, inns, forest sites) stay in French in every Latin-script
  language. Mandarin uses the form in this table, with the French name in
  brackets the first time on a page.
- Painting titles use the established title in each language.

## Banned or controlled words

| Avoid | Reason |
|---|---|
| niché, havre de paix, incontournable, joyau | brochure language |
| nestled, hidden gem, must-see, step back in time | brochure language |
| « la première réserve naturelle au monde » without hedge | contested claim; keep « ce qu'on présente souvent comme » |
| « invention de la peinture en plein air » (Corot, Barbizon) | historically wrong; open-air sketching predates them |
